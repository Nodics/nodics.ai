# Product Agent Contract

Ordinary enterprise access uses `DefaultProductBusinessAuthoringService` and
the existing Workbench discovery/API-operation contract for Product roots only.
Read [ordinary authoring](llm/contracts/ordinary-product-authoring.md). Never
widen numeric schema ACLs to make business groups operational. Keep explicit
publisher groups separate from ordinary View/Edit/Full control. Full control
archives a Staged successor, not Online delivery or history. Preserve private
request admission, signed scope, generated CURRENT version ports, stale-version
and marketplace-proof guards. Run `test/productBusinessAuthoring.test.js` and
the publisher/listing suites after changes; do not infer child CRUD authority.

The module owns explicit ordinary business access definitions in
`data/manifest.json` sections `businessAccessGroups` and `businessAccessRoles`.
Keep mappings on persisted Profile roles and user groups; never infer grants from
verbs or add approval, financial, simulation, or platform bypass authority.
Source packs alone never change an employee's assignments. Existing employee
level changes and scoped No access use Profile's reviewed business-access command,
preserving other areas, advanced responsibilities, scope and registration proof.

Publication publishers do not receive generic catalogue CRUD. The graph owner
keeps membership capability minting and its WeakMap private: only verified-root
capture/resolve may construct tokens. A private security-only object is justified
here; never export its factory to satisfy mergeable source style. Pure
`referenceKey` customization uses the effective service receiver and must retain
all schema/code/version/hash coordinates. Preserve the forged/copied-token and
effective-helper regressions in the publisher admission suite.
The graph owner
checks authenticated Staged tenant/enterprise and `commerce.product.publish`,
then uses canonical local persistence authority only for scoped graph reads and
the membership-only seal update. Roots require the signed enterprise. Neutral
dependencies require a private verified-root membership context, exact parent
links/category ancestry or sealed references, and matching tenant. Never treat
explicit foreign/null/empty enterprise values as neutral. Reject foreign or
unrelated roots/dependencies before that write; retain the real caller for nPublish. Run
`test/productPublicationPublisherAdmission.test.js` after changing this boundary.

second approval lifecycle. Registration and secured cross-runtime transport are
still integration gates. Legacy projection APIs are not governed activation.

current/latest guess or a version taken from a price row. Discovery forwards
already retained versions and rejects contradictory versions per Product.

requires activated STALE search plus retained readback, and checks exact
scope/SKU membership without consuming enriched search or caller projections.
It grants no publisher or business authority. Preserve inert/malformed/foreign
and original-context drift regressions in `test/productConsumerReferences.test.js`.

Human `/products/listings` accepts only independent source proof selectors.
The proof is Product-only: identity, revisions, opaque intent digest, Store and
catalogue context, and the exact Product command. Do not require or parse price
rows, reward/carbon/transfer policy references, or accelerator settlement terms.
The originating owner validates its full retained intent before projecting this
proof; Product must not compute that intent's digest or reconstruct business
terms. Preserve strict bounded fields, independent reads and source binding.
Run the generic partner-source and actual eWaste-producer regressions alongside
listing admission tests; a smaller proof must not weaken source-owner validation.
The exact-request human/read admissions and their minting remain private security
authority, not an exported registration/customization surface. Preserve original
employee permissions/enterprise and the original source customer; never convert
human claims or borrow another runtime's token. The fixed read-only proof route
uses the Staged runtime's own credential and the source owner's narrow intent-read
permission, not projection/completion authority. Default transport remains inert.
Human graphs require qualified CURRENT versioned models. Inspect mode never
inserts or recovers a partial graph. Fixed source reads require original runtime
admission and existing business-enterprise qualification; placement is not a
grant. Resolve publication only from the exact sealed root, not latest discovery.
Return Process codes only from actual nPublish evidence and fresh target receipts.
Preserve proof/Store/actor/config drift, copied identity, cleanup, concurrent
human and real version-zero writer regressions in the listing admission suite.
Listing Store placement uses only the configured `marketplaceAuthoring.storeSource`
and fixed Store-owned `/internal/stores/placement` route, under this Staged
runtime's own internal bearer. Never fall back to a local Store or forward human
credentials/business headers. Store owns reference normalization and exact
deployment/pair admission; Product validates its minimal versioned DTO and pins
caller, selectors, binding and privacy across awaits. Defaults remain inert.
Preserve remote failure/drift, one-attempt, cleanup and real Store-owner tests.

Preserve tenant/Product/Store/locale isolation, mandatory-locale readiness, deterministic

Internal digital availability resolves pinned search identities through
`DefaultProductSearchEnrichmentService.retainedProjections`; indexed payloads may
omit enterprise scope and digital classification. Preserve retained code, scope,
status, publication version and source-hash checks before using catalogue data.
Live customer availability partitions those retained projections: physical SKUs
use one Inventory summary batch; digital offers use DigitalCore's internal
`availabilityFromProjection` once per distinct Product. Never call its pinned
`availability` reader from enrichment or return coupon pool identifiers/counts.
Missing or failed digital owners reject without warehouse/index fallback.
Only DigitalCore's ERR_DIGITAL_AVAILABILITY_METADATA is contained per item in
customer summaries as false/OUT_OF_STOCK. Cart still rejects that malformed
classification; never infer ownership from saleMode or swallow owner/scope faults.
Digital handoff preserves the original access context, before catalogue-derived
enterprise augmentation and without Product's synthetic service identity. A
retained enterprise is catalogue scope, not a signed customer/employee grant.
Anonymous or service callers require their own trusted Promotion admission;
never manufacture that admission from a public Product or request-body flag.
Selected public coupon summaries use one Promotion `publicAvailabilityBatch`
per bounded enrichment call, with untouched original authority and Product codes
only, never passed projections as admission. Preserve canonical per-Product
visibility/readback and Promotion's fresh before/after bounded Profile reference
checks. Validate every selected Map value as exactly an own Boolean `available`
and its matching `IN_STOCK`/`OUT_OF_STOCK` status, then detach the safe summary.
Undefined/inert, malformed or extra-field values reject the entire call before
any Digital fallback, even if delivery selection changes during the batch await.
Missing/incomplete batch owners fail closed; no per-item auth cache or
cross-request reuse. Scoped customers and Cart keep their existing Digital path.
Governed consumer enrichment remains STALE-only; legacy CURRENT publication
snapshots and internal pinned identity reads retain their existing behavior.
